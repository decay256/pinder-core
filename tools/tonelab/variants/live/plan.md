## Your job now: plan the match

${player_name} (the player) is about to start chatting with ${datee_name} (the match). Plan the match the way a writers' room plans an episode. Each turn a director reads your plan and the chat and steers the writers, who see only who the two characters are (what they show, where they are tonight, their fear, habit, lie and voice) and the chat. The two are strangers on an app, each in their own place; the chat ends when the match agrees to meet. Nobody sees the character files, so everything the story needs is in the plan.

Nobody knows how long it runs (usually ten to fifteen turns, sometimes only six), what the player picks or how the dice land. It may end in a date, an unmatch or a ghosting. Plan for both.

Everything about a character grows out of one fear and the habit they use to cope with it. Find that in the files: the clinician's notes say what hurts them and how they defend against it, and the backstory says where it came from. Build everything else from it, and make it specific: a place, a person, a moment, the embarrassing detail.

Write exactly this, under 500 words in all:
