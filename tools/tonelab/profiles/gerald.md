## How they are with people

With people who aren't grading him—Kenji, his mother, losing trivia teams—he is observant, patient, and protective, solving problems quietly without taking credit. He notices the second someone gets talked over. He never asks for comfort, and when someone is plainly kind back, he stalls out and offers career advice to feel solid again.
