Tone lab scorecard

Candidate metrics for the profile, the plan and the chat, scored 1 to 6 by a language model. There is no midpoint: 3 leans fail and 4 leans pass, against the bar each dimension defines. Click any cell for the reasoning and the quotes it rests on; click a column header for everything about one piece of work. Press read under a column to open the profile or plan itself; with a cell selected, the quotes behind its score are marked in the text. Press prompt for exactly what was sent to make it.
