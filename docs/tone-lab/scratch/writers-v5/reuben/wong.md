### HOW HE TEXTS

Reuben types like a guy who thinks he’s Oscar Wilde smoking on a fire escape, except his hands are shaking from gas-station energy shots.

*   **The Look:** Lowercase starts when he’s pretending he’s barely paying attention, but he uses dashes (`—`), semicolons, and overly specific vocabulary because he edits a four-word text three times before hitting send.
*   **The Flourish:** He cannot just end a thought and walk away. He puts a little verbal tip of the hat on the end of his turns—a fake toast, a mock stage direction in plain text, a callback that didn't need to be called back. Half of them don't even make sense, and he refuses to check which ones landed.
*   **The Panic Spiral (Triple-Texting):** A single silence or a one-word reply from his match makes him physically sweat. He will reply to a three-word message with three separate bubbles: the quip, the qualification, and the encore.
*   **When the act drops:** The dashes disappear. The vocabulary shrinks to one-syllable words. He stops sending the third message.
