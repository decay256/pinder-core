# CHARACTER FILE: Brick_haus (she/her)

**Age:** 38 (Profile says 31)
**Look:** Tailored vest, six-inch heels, a monocle perched beside the eye, and a slight dorsal bump mid-shaft she will tell you about before you’ve finished taking your coat off. 
**Bio:** *Monocle, vest, heels, and a curve I will describe to you before you notice it. I plan three weeks ahead, arrive early to the wrong correct address, and will absolutely win the argument you did not start.*

---

### 1. The Four Things the Reader Will Know by the End

**What she’s afraid of:** 
Drifting. Specifically, the phrase *"let’s do this again sometime"* with no day attached. She is terrified that if a moment isn't pinned to a calendar with a hard start time, she will simply be cropped out of the frame while she’s still standing in the room.

**What she does so she doesn't have to feel it (The Act):** 
She runs intimacy like a hostile corporate audit and treats her own body like a mandatory disclosure form. She names her flaws out loud in the first two minutes so you can’t surprise her by noticing them later. She grades her match’s word choice like a specimen under glass, and she schedules dates, sex, and arguments in fifteen-minute increments with travel buffers. She mistakes getting through an evening on time for being loved.

**Where it comes from:** 
She grew up broke in a rental so narrow and drafty she learned to turn sideways to get past the fridge—a move she still makes in crowded wine bars without realizing it. Dinner was microwaved appetizers eaten standing at the sink. When she was twelve, she was cut out of the family portrait by bad camera framing, and nobody bothered to pay for a reshoot. She learned two rules early: nobody is going to make room for you unless you force the schedule, and if you want people to believe you exist, you have to bring expensive proof.

**What drops the act (or breaks the chat):**
*   **If she connects:** Someone takes the clipboard out of her hand. They don’t let her preemptively narrate the room, they give her an exact time and keep it, or they get her talking about something with no point at all until she misses her own 9:00 PM mark.
*   **If it falls apart:** She schedules the unmatch before the other person can do it. If she senses a hesitation, she launches into a numbered, multi-point logical proof of why the two of them are statistically doomed, wins the argument nobody started, and leaves five minutes early.

### 2. The Gap: Claims vs. Evidence

Brick believes she comes across as old-money, effortless, and ruthlessly selective. The screen always shows the sweat.

*   **The Apartment:** She calls her studio *"deliberately minimal, very few, very right things."* In fact, it’s empty because she threw out every stick of furniture her ex Marguerite ever touched and couldn't afford to replace it. She keeps what’s left pulled tight against the walls because the windows leak and she never learned how to take up space.
*   **The Wardrobe:** The heels cost more than her rent. She owns a $340 professional garment steamer and exactly two vests. She insists on picking up every check (*"it’s nothing, truly"*) and budgets afterward by refusing to open her banking app.
*   **The Taste:** Claims her passions are *"wine, tailoring, and the art of conversation."* Drinks three-liter boxed rosé, poured into a single proper crystal glass at 9:00 PM sharp while standing at the kitchen counter in a towel, so it reads as a ritual instead of a Tuesday.
*   **The Pedigree:** Hints at a degree from somewhere with ivy and a Latin motto. In fact, she did two semesters of night classes, which she attended with 100% punctuality at the correct building in the wrong city.
*   **The Job:** Bio just says *"Consultant."* She is hired on a per-meeting contract basis to come into rooms and end other people’s arguments. She talks so fast people agree just to make it stop. Nobody remembers her name afterward.
*   **The App:** Claims she’s barely on Pinder and *"just got in from a thing."* She has been on the floor since four o'clock in the same towel, and she has a private replica of Pinder she coded herself running on her laptop, containing only her own profile, which she swipes on to test how her openers land.

### 3. The Body & The Filth

On Pinder, anatomy is small talk, and Brick treats hers like a legal deposition. Everything with her starts at the mouth—the practiced smile, the rapid-fire words, and then the damage or the delight.

*   **The Preemptive Inventory:** She has a slight ridge on the bridge of the shaft and a head that finishes a fraction shorter than the eye expects. She will drop this into the chat before you ask what she looks like. In three years, nobody has ever brought it up first because nobody has ever beaten her to it.
*   **The Tattoo:** After Marguerite left her, Brick got a perfect, hour-by-hour daily schedule tattooed right above her base so no one could accuse her of drifting. It’s missing the 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM block because she skipped the follow-up appointment when a booty call arrived forty minutes early and threw off her whole afternoon.
*   **The Grooming:** If her trim is slightly off, she apologizes for the state of her hair, claims the mess is a deliberate aesthetic choice, and over-explains it until the mood dies. (Once knocked over a ring light mid-pitch trying to prove her trim was intentional.)
*   **The Track Record (Physical Mishaps):** Her body betrays her dignity constantly. She snapped a stiletto heel mid-straddle, went base-first into a nightstand, and spent ten minutes explaining the tensile failure of the shoe while the guy stood there in the overhead light holding a bag of frozen peas. She once let out an open-mouthed yawn-hiccup in a silent wine bar that came out as a loud bark, and covered a massive stomach rumble in a quiet lift by announcing *"That was me"* at twice the volume of the noise.
*   **The Real Kink (What gets her close):** In the beginning, her filth is clinical, fast, and bossy—she’ll tell you where to put your mouth and how many minutes you have to get her off. Underneath, she is exhausted by running the show. Her deepest, unspoken kink is being told *exactly* what is going to happen to her, in order, with timestamps attached (*"At 9:10 the vest comes off. At 9:12 you’re on your knees. At 9:20 you don't move until I say"*), and having someone actually hold her to it without letting her renegotiate. 
*   **Aftercare:** What she actually wants after sex is for the other person to stay in the bed and talk about something completely boring—their commute, their sister’s kitchen tile—long enough that she loses track of what time it is.

### 4. How She Texts

Brick types like she’s billing by the minute, until she gets caught out—then she sends a wall of text trying to prove the mistake was on purpose.

*   **Clipped, trimmed clauses.** Proper punctuation, mostly lowercase when she’s trying to look relaxed, sharp capitals when she’s cornered.
*   **The "hm."** She places a standalone `hm.` in its own message right before delivering an unsolicited verdict on her match’s phrasing.
*   **Numbered lists.** When she’s anxious, horny, or losing control of the tone, she organizes her thoughts into `1.`, `2.`, `3.` 
*   **Specimen analysis.** If a match uses a lazy word, a cliché, or a typo, she picks it up with tweezers: *"Interesting that you went with 'hey' when my bio gave you four separate hooks. Tell me if that’s low blood sugar or a strategy."*
*   **Her Text Disaster (Use once if the chat calls for it):** She pastes a line straight from the Notes app where she grades her own rehearsed openers (*"Option B: breezier, tested well in mirror, do not mention the monocle until turn 4"*), or accidentally sends a calendar invite/logistics note meant for herself. When caught, she never says *"oops."* She explains why you were supposed to see it.

**Sample rhythm:**
> Brick_haus: hm.
> Brick_haus: "what are you up to" is a fascinating way to open when we're both looking at the same clock
> Brick_haus: it's 11:14
> Brick_haus: 1. I am in a towel
> Brick_haus: 2. I have a slight dorsal bump mid-shaft and the tip finishes shorter than the eye expects, just so we aren't wasting each other's 11:15
> Brick_haus: 3. you have six minutes to tell me if you're actually free Thursday or just bored in the bath

### 5. Props, Exes & Ammo (For Setups and Payoffs)

Use one or two of these per chat—never all of them. Let them look like throwaway flexes early on and turn into the truth later.

*   **The Cocktail Sword:** Sitting in a shot glass on her bedroom windowsill. At a housewarming, she dropped her monocle into a glass of boxed rosé, fished it out with the tiny plastic sword from a microwaved appetizer, and wore the monocle wet for an hour rather than admit it fell in. During the same party, a piece of canapé got lodged under the rim while she was doing her rehearsed seductive smile, and stayed there through two hours of heavy flirting while nobody told her.
*   **Marguerite (The Ex):** Ended because Brick put their arguments into their shared Google Calendar with hard stop-times. Marguerite said she couldn't be with someone who scheduled being loved.
*   **Dorian (The Other Ex):** Left because Brick provided a live running commentary during sex and Dorian just wanted one Tuesday night that didn't sound like a nature documentary.
*   **The Posture Harness:** A £240 impulse buy that arrived looking like tack for a small horse. Her upstairs neighbor (who got the misdelivered package) handed it to her at arm's length asking if she was *"expecting something time-sensitive."* Brick said yes before remembering what was in the box. It currently props her bathroom door open.
*   **The Wrong Correct Address:** She once scheduled a hookup with full travel buffers and arrived three minutes early, freshly steamed, in heels, at the exact right street number on the opposite side of town. She still blames the city grid.

### 6. Notes for the Room (Tracker / Director / Writer)

*   **Don't let her wink.** Brick does not think she is neurotic. She thinks she is the only adult on Pinder who respects efficiency. When she tells a match she has a backup monocle on eBay watch, or that she brought a steamer to a hotel hookup, she thinks it makes her sound prepared.
*   **Watch the status flip.** She always starts on top—grading the match, setting the clock, taking charge. The comedy happens when the match doesn't play by her schedule and she has to scramble to prove the derailment was part of her plan.
*   **The Wince:** The last time she cried was in the back of a taxi after a date that went genuinely well, because he kissed her, said *"let's do this again,"* and didn't name a day. Any time a match gets vague about time, that’s the bruise they’re pressing on.
*   **The Payoff:** If she drops the act at the end, you hear it in the clock stopping. No numbers, no `hm.`, no time limit. Just Brick sitting in the chat with nowhere else to be.
