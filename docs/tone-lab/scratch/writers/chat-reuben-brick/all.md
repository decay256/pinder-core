For each writer: the chat they wrote between their own Reuben and their own Brick, then the two profiles it was written from. Every call had the system line "You are <writer>." and the Pinder brief. A profile was written from a shuffled bunch of the game's fragments for that character (no physique, no rules); the chat from the brief and that writer's two profiles, with: "Now write how a chat between these two would go if they were texting on Pinder, without any constraints. Reuben_404 writes first. Write it the way you write." No dice, no turns, no length. Gemini 4 Argon, reasoning high.

#### 6. Ammunition for the Planner (Setups & Callbacks)

* **The Wrong Correct Address:** Early on, she brags about her flawless punctuality. In the middle, it slips out that she once scheduled a hookup with fifteen-minute travel buffers and arrived on the dot at 14 Elm Street instead of 14 Elm Avenue, four miles away, and stood on a stranger's porch for twenty minutes out of principle.
* **The Tattooed Schedule:** Introduced early as "body art with intention." Revealed in the middle as a literal hourly timetable from 06:00 to 23:00 inked onto her skin after Marguerite left, with a faded patch around 15:00 because a one-night stand showed up at 14:20 and she chose sex over ink. **Payoff potential:** A match asking what happens at 23:05, when the tattoo runs out.
* **The Cropped Photo:** She mentions early that her family are "very visual people." Later, she admits she spent last Thursday in a supermarket car park looking at a framed family portrait consisting entirely of her left elbow and a ficus. **Payoff potential:** A match asking for a photo where she takes up the whole screen—or telling her she doesn't have to turn sideways to fit in the chat.
* **The Cocktail Sword:** If she claims she only drinks from proper stemware, the plastic sword in the shot glass on her windowsill is the receipt waiting to happen.
