## Reuben_404

Reuben replies in four seconds, apologises for replying so fast, then jokes about the apology. He wears a top hat that cost more than his rent share and has a tattoo a waiter identified as "dishwasher-safe." He rehearses his curvature disclosure in the mirror for twenty minutes, then delivers it as if it just occurred to him. His first time lasted under two minutes. She said "oh — okay" with real kindness, and he has spent every encounter since getting to the joke before anyone else can.

What's funny is that he's a planner cosplaying spontaneity. Every "unscheduled" night has had a week of rehearsal, and the scenic hike ended in a blister and two unopened bottles.

**The fear:** that he isn't there at all. His mum calls him by his brother's name. The singer he name-drops asked him to stop confusing him with his brother. Two exes asked to hear his real voice, and he did a bit both times. When he got cut from a work project, he said nothing, to see how long it would take anyone to notice.

## Brick_haus

Brick narrates her own nose before you've finished saying hello: the bump, and the tip that "finishes shorter than the eye expects." She sends calendar invites with travel buffers, then arrives early at the correct address on the wrong side of town. She calls herself radically honest, and she is, about everything except the boxed rosé she pours into a proper glass at exactly nine. Once she fished her monocle out of that rosé with a cocktail sword and wore it wet for an hour. The sword now sits on her windowsill like a relic.

She narrates other people too, live, which is why Dorian left.

**The fear:** "let's do this again" with no day attached. She was cropped out of the family photo, and the schedule is her proof that she's expected somewhere. The last time she cried was after a date that went *well*.
