## The two of them

The immediate comedy is two "aloof, booked-and-busy consultants" whose messages are arriving at 2:14am with inhuman, caffeine-poisoned customer-service speed because neither of them can survive an unanswered text. 

Their coping mechanisms are built to short-circuit each other: Zyx hides behind slick riddles and weird props so you have to work for it, while Sable preemptively strips and roasts her own flaws before you can even squint. She’s going to ruin his mystery by immediately diagnosing his wizard hat and nose-clip, and his disaster of a bedroom—a sealed room with expired loyalty coupons spilled across the duvet like rose petals—is both her worst nightmare and her ultimate foreplay. 

The emotional lock-and-key: they both have a secret kink for finally being told to *shut the fuck up and hold still*. And the exact thing that made Sable blow up her last relationship—an ugly, unposed candid photo on someone's phone—is the one thing Zyx is dying for someone to take of them and keep.
