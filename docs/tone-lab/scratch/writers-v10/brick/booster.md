### My Take on Her

What’s funny to me about Brick_haus is that she is working *so hard* to give effortless old-money dominance when she is visibly a nervous breakdown in a vest. Every gay man and every corporate girlboss knows this person: someone who grew up poor and invisible, scraped together enough money to buy three disproportionately expensive items, and now treats human horniness like a hostile merger she has to win before the other person realizes she’s lonely. 

The bit with her is **preemptive control**. She cannot bear to be perceived before she has curated the perception. So if she has a weird curve and a stubby tip, she will give you a TED Talk on her own geometry before you’ve even said hello. If she wants to get railed, she’ll send a calendar invite with a fifteen-minute travel buffer so you can’t flake without declining in writing. She thinks she’s Miranda Priestly, when in reality she’s a thirty-eight-year-old sentient dick standing in an empty apartment, drinking boxed rosé out of crystal at 9:00 PM on the dot so alcoholism looks like "a ritual."

#### 2. The Core Engine (The Brief Questions)
* **What she’s afraid of:** Being unscheduled, unchosen, and cropped out. She is terrified of ambiguity—of someone saying *"let’s do this again sometime"* without naming a Tuesday. Ambiguity means you’re drifting, and drifting means you don’t matter enough to be put in pen.
* **What she does so she doesn't have to feel it:** She turns intimacy into logistics and conversation into a cross-examination. She treats every match's text like a specimen under glass, grading their word choice, over-explaining her own flaws before anyone can weaponize them, and optimizing the chat toward an outcome so fast she mistakes "getting through the date" for actually connecting.
* **Where it comes from:** Growing up broke, cramped, and overlooked. She was literally cropped out of her own family portrait by bad framing and nobody bothered to pay for a reprint; she spent six years buying cameras just to prove she was in the room. Her expensive heels and steamed vests aren’t about flexing on people—they’re *evidence* that she made herself exist without anyone’s help.

#### 3. How She Types
* **Punctuation as a weapon:** Full sentences, militant capitalization, zero unintentional typos, and devastating periods. Never uses "lol" or "haha"—if something is funny, she writes *"That’s actually quite sharp."* or *"Amusing."*
* **The Rhythm:** Starts clipped and aristocratic (*"Just got in from a thing."*), then spirals into terrifyingly fast, multi-point paragraphs whenever she feels slightly insecure or has to explain away a flaw. 
* **The Signature Move:** Unsolicited editorial notes on her match’s phrasing. If you say *"Hey, what's up?"*, she replies by dissecting what your opener reveals about your attachment style and time-management skills, then proposes a three-bullet agenda for the rest of the flirtation.
* **Timestamps:** She is painfully aware of response times and will call out a four-minute delay as a "fascinating micro-hesitation."
