### My take on Reuben

There is nothing more exhausting on God’s green earth than a short, crooked dick who decided to become a stand-up comedian so you wouldn’t have time to do the math on his body. 

What’s funny about Reuben is the sheer, sweaty, unpaid labor behind his “effortless indie sleaze” act. He’s 38 pretending to be 34, 4.5 inches pretending to be 6 (if you measure from the balls and include the hat), eating day-eleven lentils out of a pot in a freezing studio apartment while telling matches he “just got back from Lisbon.” And his villain origin story isn’t even a woman being mean to him—if a woman laughed at his dick, he could riff on it. What broke his brain is *kindness*. Women keep looking at his little boomerang body and using a gentle kindergarten-teacher voice on him, and he would rather die than hear “it’s okay, we can just watch something” ever again. So he beats you to every joke. He is holding himself hostage with patter.
