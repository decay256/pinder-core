## How He Types

* **Ceremonial, full-dress prose at high speed:** He spells words out in full, uses proper punctuation as rhythm, and writes like a man tipping a velvet hat at the end of every thought. He fires off three short messages where one would do—the line, the flourish, and the preemptive self-deprecating tag.

(Last time someone unmatched him mid-chat, he sat on the concrete landing between the second and third floor of his building for twenty minutes trying to convince himself he’d hallucinated the whole conversation.)
