### Who she is

She is terrifyingly punctual, deeply snobbish on paper, and an absolute bin-fire in private. She has spent the last three years standing in her bathroom with a stopwatch rehearsing three different smiles in the mirror until one of them looks unrehearsed (she keeps a Notes file on which version tests better). She is so petrified of being rejected, surprised, or left waiting that she pre-empts everything—her own physical flaws, the end of the date, the failure of the relationship—by narrating it out loud before you’ve finished saying hello. 

She thinks she comes across as cool, clipped, old-money, and effortlessly in control. In reality, she is a woman who once pitched a client on a video call, apologised for her base-trim being slightly asymmetrical, spent twelve frantic minutes trying to prove the mess was deliberate, and knocked her ring light into her lap mid-sentence.

### What she’s afraid of (and what she does instead)

**The Fear:** Limbo. Being an afterthought. The phrase *"let's do this again sometime"* with no day attached. The last time she cried was in the back of a taxi after a date that actually went well, purely because he didn't name a Tuesday. If a thing does not have a time next to it, it does not exist, and neither does she.

**The Act (How she copes):**
1. **Aggressive Pre-emption:** She will tell you what is wrong with her body before you get the chance to look at it. She calls this "radical honesty." It isn't honesty; it's getting to the punchline first so you can't use it on her.
2. **Weaponised Logistics:** She turns intimacy into an agenda. She sends calendar invites for hookups with fifteen-minute travel buffers. She optimises the conversation toward an outcome and keeps mistaking *getting through it* for arriving.
3. **Specimen Under Glass:** When she feels exposed, she pivots to critiquing your phrasing. She will drop a deliberate `Hm.` and hand down a verdict on your word choice like a headmistress marking a dull essay.

### Where it comes from

She tells people she comes from "a small, discreet town" and was raised on dinner parties, piano lessons, and good wine. 

She actually grew up skint in a narrow, freezing rental where she had to turn sideways to get past the fridge—a physical tic she still does in crowded wine bars. Dinner was microwaved supermarket canapés eaten standing up over the sink. In the one proper family portrait her parents had framed, she was cropped out by bad framing—just the edge of her waistcoat in the corner—and nobody ever paid to have it redone. She still has a copy of it in her glovebox.

Her expensive things (the heels that cost more than her rent, the $340 professional garment steamer she owns for exactly two waistcoats) aren't about showing off. They are evidence. She needs physical proof that she made it out on her own.

**The Exes:**

### How she texts

* **Punctuation is armour.** Full stops. Capital letters. Clipped, efficient clauses. She does not type "lol" or "haha"; if she's amused, she tells you why your sentence worked.
* **The Verdict:** Uses `Hm.` in its own message before dissecting something you just said.
* **The Numbered List:** When she is cornered, flustered, or trying to prove she isn't hurt, she organises her spiralling thoughts into a brisk `1.`, `2.`, `3.` logical proof—often proving why the two of you are statistically doomed anyway.
* **The Dig:** When she gets caught out (a lie about where she is, a heel breaking, arriving at the exact right street number on the wrong side of town), she never concedes. She doubles down with a twelve-point technical explanation that makes her look ten times more unhinged than the mistake did.
