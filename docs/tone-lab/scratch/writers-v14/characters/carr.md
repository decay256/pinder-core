## Reuben_404

He’s four-and-a-half inches with a hard left curve, a top hat that cost more than his rent, and a kanji tattoo that means *dishwasher-safe*. His act is the effortless, off-the-grid raconteur—*"only on here when it rains"*—except he replies in four seconds flat because he’s been indoors for eleven days eating lentils out of a pot. He’s an out-of-work coder whose only finished product is a Pinder clone with one user—himself—and his own algorithm rejected him at 3am.

He’s terrified of a quiet room. His first time ended under two minutes with a woman saying *"oh—okay"* with devastating kindness; his last ex, Delphine, left because he couldn’t say one sentence that wasn't a bit. He rehearses his "spontaneous" banter and curvature disclaimer in the bathroom mirror because if he stops tap-dancing for ten seconds, he thinks he disappears.

## Brick_haus

She’s a dick in skyscraper heels, a monocle, and one of two vests she maintains with a $340 industrial steamer. She has her daily schedule tattooed on her skin so nobody can accuse her of drifting. She’s a contract dispute-ender who treats flirting like a hostile merger: dissecting your word choice and delivering a pre-emptive audit of her own bump and shorter-than-expected tip before you’ve said hello. Behind the "curated minimalist" bio, her flat is empty because she binned everything her ex touched, and she fishes her monocle out of boxed rosé with a plastic cocktail sword.

She’s terrified of *"let’s do this again sometime."* Her ex Marguerite left because she put their arguments in a shared calendar with hard stop-times. She cries in taxis over unscheduled second dates, because if love isn't locked into a calendar slot with travel buffers, she assumes she’s already been left.

## The two of them

On paper, it’s a car crash: a fake-spontaneous bullshitter meets a woman with a timetable tattooed on her shaft. He’ll try his rehearsed, hat-tipping charm; she’ll clock his four-second reply time and dissect his syntax like a coroner. They’ll play pre-emptive-strike chicken over whose curve is worse.

The bit to build on is **contractual spontaneity**. Reuben’s secret kink is having his mouth taken away so he can't do the voice or the joke; Brick_haus’s is someone actually sticking to the schedule. When she aggressively itemises their hypothetical night to call his bluff—*20:00 drinks, 20:14 he loses at arm-wrestling, 21:00 sex, 21:05 ten minutes where he is contractually forbidden to speak or be funny*—it accidentally becomes the dirtiest talk he’s ever heard. She gives him an order to shut up and just exist; he gives her a calendar invite with an actual time on it. Their neuroses interlock like Lego.
