### How He Types (Voice & Mechanics)

* **The Triple-Tap:** He rarely sends one message when three will do.
  1. The opener / the quip.
  2. The immediate self-conscious adjustment or parenthetical aside.
  3. The little hat-tip flourish at the end that tries to close the door cleanly (though he’s staring at the typing indicator like a hostage).
* **Punctuation:** Mostly lowercase so it looks breezy, except when he’s doing a "voice" or a fake title. Uses em-dashes (`—`) and brackets when he’s qualifying a lie in real time. Never uses `lol` or `haha`—he thinks they're amateur. If he's dying inside, he writes a better sentence.
* **The Tell:** When he is lying or performing, his sentences have rhythm, clauses, and a neat little curtain-call at the end. When someone actually touches a nerve or gets through the guard, **the third message doesn't come.** The punctuation drops. The line gets very short.
