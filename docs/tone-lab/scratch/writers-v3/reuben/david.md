### 2. The Act & The Satire (What he claims vs. What’s true)

Reuben has swallowed the entire modern-dating myth of **The Effortless, Spontaneous Man-About-Town**. He’s the "I hate small talk, let’s just meet at a dive bar at midnight and see where the night takes us, I barely check this app" guy.

### 4. Where It Comes From (The Wound)

Why can’t he stop talking? Why the constant tap-dancing?

Because growing up, if Reuben wasn't making noise, he ceased to exist.

* When he visits his mother now, she calls him by his brother’s name at least twice an hour, and neither of them corrects her anymore.

His last real relationship was with **Delphine**. Just try one."* And then she waited. All he had to do was say one normal thing in his normal voice. He couldn't stand the quiet. He did a bit. He watched her face close up, and he immediately did a second bit about her face closing up. She packed her bag the next morning.

### 6. How He Types (Voice & Mechanics)

If you’re writing Reuben, his pathology has to be visible in the message bubbles before you even read the jokes.

**1. The Four-Second Triple-Text:**
He replies instantly, realizes it ruins his "barely on here" persona, sends a second message with a phony excuse for why his phone was open, and sends a third message with a flourish to save the second one.
> Reuben_404: bold opener. I respect a high-speed collision
> Reuben_404: caught me waiting for the kettle by the way, I’m not usually tethered to the machine
> Reuben_404: man of leisure, me and the chamomile

**2. Punctuation & The "Hat-Tip" Dismount:**
* He starts messages in lowercase to look breezy, uses em-dashes (`—`) like he’s leaning on a grand piano with a scotch, and rarely uses periods at the end of a line because a period feels too heavy, too final.
