## Zyx_444

Zyx wears a wizard hat to bed, a clip-on nose piece that a date once mistook for a medical device, and a rose tattoo commemorating a bouquet from a secret admirer that was actually delivered to the widow upstairs by mistake. They claim to be a mystery-shrouded "conversational dominance" consultant; in reality, they work 2am live-chat support for a coupon app, paid per resolved ticket, which is why they text like a hostage negotiator on speed. They once finished in ninety seconds and immediately delivered an eleven-minute tight five so she’d still tell her friends they were "great in bed."

They grew up in a freezing house where you spoke fast or got cropped out of the family photo. Zyx is terrified of a five-second silence—the moment the patter stops and someone asks a plain question like *do you want me to stay?*—because stopping means disappearing.

## Sable_xo

Sable is a self-styled "personal brand strategist" Held Together By Debt: a £212 ring light on a floor mattress, dry-scooped pre-workout at midnight, and clip-in hair extensions on a 29% credit card she calls her "marketing spend." Her armour is beating you to the insult—she announces her own asymmetry within four minutes of getting undressed so her mouth says it before your face does. She once bought a $94 French maid costume for a match’s fantasy, noticed the limescale in his bathtub, and scrubbed it for an hour until he went soft and tipped her twenty quid.

Her mother still critiques her profile photos like a returns desk. Sable is terrified of being seen unedited. She dumped the one man who loved an unposed photo of her asleep, and she wakes at 5am after hookups to lint-roll her own shed hair fibers off the pillowcase before he opens his eyes.

## The two of them

It’s an arms race of pre-emptive self-deprecation between two people who both talk to their own dating profiles out loud at 3am. 

The bit to build on is the speed and the staging. Zyx fires off riddles at 140 words a minute because they’re tab-switching from the coupon-app helpdesk; Sable is rehearsing both sides of the chat out loud in her bathroom, deleting her reply twice, and firing it back in four seconds so she looks effortless. They’re both trying to win the conversation before they can be rejected in it. 

Where they collide is the mess: Zyx is a compulsive talker hiding a bedroom full of expired loyalty coupons who secretly wants to be gagged and photographed badly; Sable is a compulsive cleaner who deleted a man for photographing her badly and just wants someone to hold her wrists still until she stops performing.
