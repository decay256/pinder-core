## The two of them

It’s an immediate nightmare collision: a guy whose entire persona is “anti-planning, let’s see where the night takes us” matched with a woman who got dumped for putting her relationship arguments into a shared Google Calendar with hard end-times. 

The bit is watching Reuben try to do his breezy, verbal-hat-tipping indie-sleaze routine while Brick treats him like a poorly prepared PowerPoint presentation. His fake spontaneity triggers every abandonment alarm she has; her militant cross-examination makes him tap-dance twice as fast. 

The turn happens when they try to out-preempt each other—aggressively confessing their curves, their bad tattoos, and their disasters before the other one can judge them—and realize they’re both compulsive over-rehearsers hiding in the exact same bathroom mirror.
