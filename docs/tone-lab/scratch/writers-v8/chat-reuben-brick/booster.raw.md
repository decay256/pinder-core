## The joke

The joke is an arms race between two people running the exact same defence—spoiling the inspection before the other person can squint at their photos—in opposite fonts. In the setup, both try to pre-emptively reject themselves within seconds of matching at 1:14 a.m.: Reuben pretends to be an unscheduled libertine ("drinks sometime") while speed-roasting his 4.5-inch left hook behind a vaudeville sign-off so nobody can pity him, and Brick puts `"Sometime."` on trial, counters with a numbered inventory of her six-inch left curve, and demands a clock so she cannot be drifted out on. It turns when Brick accidentally pastes her timestamped date-prep notes into the chat and tries to pass them off as a client template, cracking both of their acts open—her scheduling fights with her ex until she got left and drinking boxed rosé at 9:00 sharp to survive an unscheduled kiss, him doing a non-stop bit ever since a girl met his two-minute finish with gentle pity and his ex begged for one sentence in his normal voice. The punchline is that their defences are each other's exact kink: the man who claims he doesn't do schedules takes her clipboard away, gives her a filthy minute-by-minute itinerary that physically shuts his own mouth so he can't do the voice, and gets both of them to drop their punctuation armour for an actual Tuesday.
